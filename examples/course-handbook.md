# Applied AI Course Handbook — September 2026

## Course structure
The Applied AI course consists of four live sessions. Each session lasts two hours.
The course teaches routing, planning, tool use, retrieval-augmented generation, execution,
reflection, and multi-agent orchestration. The instructor is Dr. Mira Sen.
The maximum cohort size is 30 students. The course is taught in English.

## Fees and tax
The base course fee is INR 12,000 per student. Goods and Services Tax (GST) is 18% of the
base fee and is charged in addition to the base fee. No group discount is offered.
For this handbook's examples, apply GST after any explicitly stated scholarship deduction.

## Refund policy
A cancellation at least seven calendar days before the first session receives an 80%
refund of the base course fee. GST treatment is handled separately by the billing team;
the handbook does not specify whether GST is refundable. Cancellations fewer than seven
calendar days before the first session receive no refund. Requests go to the course coordinator.

## Assessment and submission
The capstone is worth 25 marks: 15 marks for code and 10 marks for the video explanation.
Students must build a multi-agent RAG system with at least five agents and evaluate it with
at least 30 queries. The submission includes a public GitHub repository, detailed README,
architecture diagram, setup instructions, test cases and results, and a YouTube demonstration.
The capstone deadline is 25 September 2026 at 17:58 India Standard Time.

## Attendance and support
Students must attend at least three of the four sessions to receive a completion certificate.
Recordings are available for 90 days after the final session. Teaching support answers course
questions from Monday to Friday, 10:00–18:00 India Standard Time. No weekend support is promised.

## Scope of this document
This handbook does not specify placement guarantees, instructor salary, campus parking fees,
scholarship eligibility, or student banking information. Do not assume such policies exist.
